# Bedtime Stories Resources

Free, open resources to help parents navigate bedtime routines, AI-generated content for kids, and healthy sleep habits.

## What's Here

- **[AI Story Safety Checklist](https://loran-code.github.io/bedtime-stories-resources/)** - A practical checklist for evaluating AI-powered story apps before letting your child use them. Covers content safety, data privacy, AI transparency, quality signals, and healthy usage.

## Why This Exists

AI story generators for kids are popping up everywhere. Some are built responsibly. Others are general-purpose AI tools slapped with a "for kids" label. Parents deserve a clear way to tell the difference.

These resources are free to use, share, and adapt.

## About

Created by [Loran](https://www.bedtime-stories.fun/about), a parent and developer behind [Bedtime Stories](https://www.bedtime-stories.fun), an AI-powered personalized story platform for children ages 3-12.
